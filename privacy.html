Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Kilat Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool that posts short language-learning videos to TikTok and YouTube accounts owned by its developer. This page explains what data the app uses.

1. Data the app receives

  • TikTok: your basic profile (open ID, username, display name) and the access tokens needed to post videos (scopes user.info.basic and video.publish).
  • YouTube: your channel name and the access tokens needed to upload videos.

The app does not read your messages, followers, contacts, viewing history or any other personal data.

2. How the data is used

The data is used only to upload your own videos and captions to your own account, and to show which account is connected.

3. Where the data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the developer's own computer. They are not sent to any server other than TikTok's and Google's own APIs, and are never sold or shared with third parties.

4. Removing your data

You can revoke access at any time: in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services, or in your Google account under Third-party apps & services. You can also ask for deletion by email, and the stored tokens will be deleted.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
